About Me
Lighting is where a scene gets its emotion, focus & depth.

I'm J. Duryodhan Reddy, a fresher 3D Lighting Artist based in Berhampur, Odisha. I focus on cinematic and environment lighting, working primarily in Maya and Arnold with additional lighting studies in Blender and Cycles. My process moves from key/fill/rim relationships and material response through render passes and basic compositing — always aiming for readable, believable and emotionally grounded frames.
