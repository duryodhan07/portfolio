About

I'm J. Duryodhan Reddy, a fresher 3D Lighting Artist based in Berhampur, Odisha, focused on cinematic and environment lighting. I work primarily in Maya and Arnold, with working knowledge of Nuke and Blender — building mood, depth and believability through light across characters, environments and still-life studies.

My process runs from blocking key/fill/rim relationships through AOV-based render passes to a finished frame in basic compositing. Certified in 3D & VFX through ZICA Odisha, I'm currently building toward a junior lighting role at a VFX, animation or games studio.

Nightwatch

A night-interior character study built around a single warm practical source. A desk lamp motivates the key across the creature's fur and window glass, while a cool exterior ambience separates the subject from the room behind it. A CRT television supplies a secondary flicker source, adding a second colour note and a small piece of storytelling in the reflection.

Scene 02 — Beauty

Candlelight Study

A character lighting study built around key, fill and rim, focused on shape, separation and mood. A candle flame drives a warm, low-angle key across the face and hands, a soft fill opens the shadow side, and a glowing backlight rims the hair to separate the character from the wall behind. Foreground books frame the shot and control depth.

Shallow depth of field and a softly bokeh'd background of warm and cool practicals push attention to the clock face while keeping the wider space believable.
